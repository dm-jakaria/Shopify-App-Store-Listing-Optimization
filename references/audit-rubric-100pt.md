# 100-Point Shopify App Store Listing Audit Rubric

This comprehensive rubric provides a quantitative, battle-tested framework for diagnosing any Shopify App Store listing across 5 key pillars.

---

## Pillar 1: Positioning & Value Proposition (25 Points)

| Criteria | Max Pts | Assessment Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Immediate Problem-Solution Clarity** | 6 pts | Can a merchant understand *what* the app does and *who* it is for within 5 seconds of scanning the listing? |
| **Outcome-Focused vs. Feature Dump** | 5 pts | Does the copy emphasize merchant ROI, revenue, or hours saved, rather than dry technical specs? |
| **Merchant Persona Fit** | 5 pts | Is the language tailored to the target segment (e.g., dropshippers, high-volume apparel, B2B wholesale)? |
| **Differentiation from Competitors** | 5 pts | Is there a distinct angle or clear reason to pick this app over established market leaders? |
| **Readability & Formatting** | 4 pts | Use of concise paragraphs, bold headers, clear bullet points, and scannable visual rhythm. |

---

## Pillar 2: Search Visibility & ASO (25 Points)

| Criteria | Max Pts | Assessment Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **App Title Optimization** | 7 pts | Contains primary high-intent keyword; under 30 characters (no mobile truncation); natural brand styling. |
| **Subtitle / Tagline Indexing** | 6 pts | Strictly within 63 characters; includes secondary keyword; clear emotional or commercial hook. |
| **Keyword Density & Distribution** | 5 pts | Natural semantic distribution of core terms across benefits and description without keyword stuffing. |
| **Search Intent Coverage** | 4 pts | Addresses both direct category queries (e.g., `preorder app`) and symptom queries (`out of stock alert`). |
| **Policy & Trademark Compliance** | 3 pts | Zero unauthorized use of "Shopify" or banned superlative terms ("#1", "Best"). |

---

## Pillar 3: Visual Assets & Screenshot Storyboard (20 Points)

| Criteria | Max Pts | Assessment Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **App Icon Brand Recognition** | 4 pts | Clean 1200x1200px icon, legible at 48x48px on mobile, no cluttered text or forbidden Shopify logos. |
| **Slide 1 Hook Power** | 5 pts | First screenshot delivers a compelling visual punch, clear value headline, and immediate context. |
| **Narrative Flow & Progression** | 4 pts | Slides follow a structured journey: Hook -> Ease of Setup -> Core Feature -> ROI -> Trust. |
| **Annotation Readability on Mobile** | 4 pts | Large, high-contrast headline text overlays that can be read effortlessly on smartphone screens. |
| **Native Admin Representation** | 3 pts | Shows clean Polaris UI / App Bridge elements indicating high quality and low learning curve. |

---

## Pillar 4: Pricing Architecture & Risk Reversal (15 Points)

| Criteria | Max Pts | Assessment Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Free Tier / Trial Clarity** | 5 pts | Transparent trial duration (e.g., 14-day free trial) or clear "Free forever" tier conditions. |
| **Tier Distinction & Scalability** | 4 pts | Logical value progression between tiers (order volume, features, or store staff limits). |
| **Zero Hidden Cost Transparency** | 3 pts | Upfront disclosure of any transaction fees, usage overages, or required third-party APIs. |
| **Risk-Reversal Copy** | 3 pts | Clear statements on instant cancellation, no credit card required to install, or money-back guarantees. |

---

## Pillar 5: Social Proof & Trust Engineering (15 Points)

| Criteria | Max Pts | Assessment Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Theme Safety & Speed Reassurance** | 4 pts | Explicitly promises 2.0 App Block integration, zero liquid code pollution, and sub-50ms speed impact. |
| **Customer Support Commitments** | 4 pts | Explicit support availability stated (e.g., "24/7 live chat, <5 minute response time"). |
| **Review Strategy & Quote Integration** | 4 pts | Strategically quotes specific merchant wins or verified case study metrics within the copy. |
| **"Built for Shopify" Alignment** | 3 pts | Mentions or demonstrates compliance with Polaris standards and native merchant experience. |

---

## Scoring Scale & Action Plan

| Total Score | Rating Grade | Action Plan |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **90 - 100** | **Elite (Tier 1)** | High-converting listing. Focus on review velocity, localized translations, and featured promotions. |
| **75 - 89** | **Strong (Tier 2)** | Solid foundation. Minor tweaks in keyword targeting, screenshot copy, or pricing clarity will yield quick wins. |
| **55 - 74** | **Mediocre (Tier 3)** | Significant conversion leakage. Needs overhaul of title/subtitle keywords, benefit bullets, and screenshot flow. |
| **< 55** | **Critical (Tier 4)** | Major overhaul required. High probability of low search rank, high bounce rate, and high uninstalls. |
