# Shopify App Store Listing Guidelines & Compliance Reference

This document outlines official constraints, character limits, policy rules, and formatting best practices for publishing and optimizing listings on the Shopify App Store.

---

## 1. Character Limits & Field Specifications

| Asset / Field | Character Limit | Formatting Rules | Key Optimization Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **App Name** | **30 characters** (recommended)<br>*Hard limit: 50 characters* | Plain text. Alphanumeric + standard punctuation (`:`, `-`). | Primary keyword placement. Avoid mobile truncation. |
| **App Subtitle / Tagline** | **63 characters** (hard limit) | Plain text. No emoji spam or all-caps. | High search algorithmic weight. Clear UVP (Unique Value Proposition). |
| **Key Benefits (3-5)** | **Title**: 30-40 chars<br>**Description**: 100-140 chars | Bullet style. Action-oriented verbs. | Merchant ROI, time saved, revenue increased. |
| **Detailed Description** | **Up to 2,800 characters** | Markdown supported (H2, H3, bold, lists, links). | Problem-Agitate-Solve framework, integrations, technical FAQs. |
| **Search Keywords** | **5 keywords / phrases** in Partner Dashboard | Plain text tags. | High-intent, non-duplicated search terms. |
| **Pricing Plan Descriptions** | **Up to 100 characters** per bullet point | Bullet points under each tier. | Features, usage limits, overage transparency. |

## 2. Policy & Compliance Restrictions

The Shopify App Review Team strictly enforces app listing policies. Violations will cause listing rejection or unpublishing:

### A. Trademark & Brand Identity
- **Never use "Shopify" in the app title** unless formatted strictly as `[App Name] for Shopify` (and even then, this wastes valuable title characters; it is better to omit it).
- **Never use the Shopify shopping bag icon or official logos** in your app icon, banners, or screenshots.
- **Never imply official partnership, endorsement, or ownership** by Shopify unless you have formal written authorization.

### B. Prohibited Superlatives & Unsubstantiated Claims
- Strictly forbidden without official documentation:
  - "The #1 App for..."
  - "The Best App in the Store"
  - "Officially recommended by Shopify"
  - "Guaranteed 10x sales boost"
- Replace superlatives with verifiable capabilities:
  - *Instead of:* "The #1 automated backup app"
  - *Use:* "Automated real-time daily store & product backups"

### C. Pricing Transparency
- If your app requires external subscription billing or third-party API keys (e.g., OpenAI, Twilio), this must be prominently disclosed in the listing description and pricing section.
- Free plans must provide genuine ongoing functionality, not just a gated trial disguised as a free tier.

## 3. Formatting & Readability Rules for Descriptions

To ensure high conversion rates on both desktop and mobile:

1. **Scannable Headers**: Use H2 and H3 markdown tags to break sections into digestible chunks.
2. **Short Paragraphs**: No paragraph should exceed 3 lines on mobile (~40-50 words).
3. **Outcome-Driven Bullets**: Begin feature bullets with bold benefit tags:
   - `**Automated Delivery Tracking:** Keep customers informed with branded SMS & email alerts.`
   - `**Zero Liquid Theme Edits:** Installs via 2.0 App Blocks with zero lingering code upon uninstall.`
4. **Theme Compatibility Badge**: Explicitly mention compatibility with Online Store 2.0 and headless storefronts (Hydrogen).
5. **Human Support Guarantee**: State response time expectations (e.g., "Live 24/7 chat support with <5 min average response").
